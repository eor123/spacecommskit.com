SCK-2400-M
The SCK-2400 Radio, Ready to Solder Onto Your Board

The same RF path as the SCK-2400 (CC2652P, +20 dBm high-power TX) in a much smaller module. No power supply and no headers: power in, UART0, UART1, SPI, I2C, JTAG and spare GPIO come out on castellated edge pads along its two long edges, so you solder it directly onto your own custom board and get AES-128-CCM encrypted CCSDS packets and over-air firmware updates in under 25 seconds.

SCK-2400-M at a Glance

A fully programmable CC2652P platform in module form. Our open source (GPLv3) firmware is a working starting point. Program the module over JTAG and change it for your own use case. Hardware design is proprietary.

Which One?

SCK-2400 or SCK-2400-M?

Same firmware, same SCK Ground Station (SCK-2400 mode), same SCK-PBL-1 payload bridge over UART. Pick the SCK-2400 board for development, bench work and HAB/LEO flights; pick the SCK-2400-M when you are building your own board and want the radio soldered right onto it.
